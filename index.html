Privacy policy

Your lyrics. Your PC. A secure local connection.

App: LiveSetPro Remote

Developer: MARINESCU DANIEL

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Privacy contact: livesetxpro@gmail.com

LiveSetPro Remote is the Android companion to LiveSetPro for Windows. It displays lyrics and playback information from the Windows PC you pair with. The Android app has no account registration, advertising, analytics, developer-operated cloud storage or automatic diagnostic-upload service. We do not sell app data.

Camera and QR pairing

Camera access is requested when you choose Scan QR. Camera frames are processed on your phone to read the pairing QR. The app does not save photos or video, upload camera frames or access your photo library. The QR contains the PC connection details, a temporary authentication credential and the fingerprint of the PC session certificate. A Direct QR also contains local Wi-Fi connection details.

Wi-Fi and Android permissions

The app uses network access to connect to your PC. When you choose Direct pairing, Android 13 and later uses the Nearby devices permission. Android 10–12 instead requires foreground Location permission and system Location to approve this Wi-Fi connection. The app does not read location coordinates, track your movements or send your location. It requests no background Location, microphone or storage permission. On older supported Android versions, use a shared local network.

Local communication

The app sends the pairing token to your chosen LiveSetPro PC for local authentication and receives song titles, artist information, lyrics and playback timing from that PC. These messages are not sent to a developer-owned server. The Wi-Fi name and password from a Direct QR are passed to Android's Wi-Fi connection API, not to a developer server. Playback controls and song audio stay on the PC.

Remote uses an encrypted local TLS/WebSocket connection. The QR or complete secure pairing code verifies the exact PC session certificate before the app sends its authentication token. Protect the pairing QR/code: someone who obtains the current code can use that session. Stopping Remote on the PC invalidates the session, and a new start creates fresh credentials and a new certificate. Direct works without Internet access; there is no cloud or public certificate-service dependency. Pairing uses numeric local IP addresses, without hostname lookup. Android, Google Play and your network provider operate independently under their own policies.

Storage and retention

Pairing credentials and received song information are held in app memory; the app does not write them to a database, preferences, saved instance state or files. The app disables its data backup and device-transfer data extraction. Going to the background closes the active connection and releases any Direct network request. Direct pairing may remain in memory so the app can reconnect when you return. Disconnect, ending the app process, or clearing its ViewModel releases the session credentials. Re-pair after process termination. There is no developer-held account or cloud data to delete.

Your choices

You can cancel scanning, disconnect at any time, and revoke Camera, Nearby devices or Location permission in Android Settings. Without Camera you can paste the complete secure pairing code. Copying this code on Windows places credentials on the system clipboard at your request; protect it as you would the QR. Without the permission required for Direct, you can use a shared local network.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact livesetxpro@gmail.com. This policy describes the Android app; your Windows music library stays under your control. Future changes to the app's data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date.

Support and privacy requests

For help with LiveSetPro Remote or a privacy question, email livesetxpro@gmail.com. Include your Android version, app version and a description of the issue. Do not send pairing QR codes, Remote tokens, Wi-Fi passwords or private song content.

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so we can respond. This correspondence is separate from the app's local Remote session and remains in the email system used to handle your request. You can use the same address to request deletion of support correspondence; any applicable retention obligations will be explained in response.

About this website

This static website has no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, forms or cookies added by us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes website requests and logs visitors' IP addresses for security, including when visitors are not signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Website hosting is separate from the app's local phone-to-PC connection.
