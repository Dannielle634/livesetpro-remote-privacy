Privacy policy

Your lyrics. Your PC. A secure local connection.

App: LiveSetPro Remote

Developer: MARINESCU DANIEL

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Privacy contact: livesetxpro@gmail.com

LiveSetPro Remote is the Android companion to LiveSetPro for Windows. It displays lyrics and Player/playback information from the Windows PC you pair with. With compatible Android and Windows versions and the Windows user's permission, Android can request playback changes, Repeat settings, LiveSetPro Windows volume changes and Karaoke Follow on/off. Windows remains the playback authority: song audio plays on Windows and is not streamed to Android. Older compatible hosts may provide lyrics and playback information without controls.

The Android app has no account registration, advertising, analytics, developer-operated cloud storage or automatic diagnostic-upload service. We do not sell app data.

Camera and QR pairing

Camera access is requested when you choose Scan QR. Camera frames are processed on your phone to read the pairing QR. The app does not save photos or video, upload camera frames or access your photo library. The QR contains the PC connection details, a temporary authentication credential and the fingerprint of the PC session certificate. A Direct QR also contains local Wi-Fi connection details.

Wi-Fi and Android permissions

The app uses network access to connect to your PC. When you choose Direct pairing, Android 13 and later uses the Nearby devices permission. Android 10–12 instead requires foreground Location permission and system Location to approve this Wi-Fi connection. The app does not read location coordinates, track your movements or send your location. It requests no background Location, microphone or storage permission. On older supported Android versions, use a shared local network.

Local communication

Windows sends the paired Android device song titles, artist information, lyrics, playback timing/status, Player state, Repeat and Shuffle state where supported, volume/mute state, Previous/Next availability, Karaoke Follow state and the results of control requests.

Android sends local authentication/session messages and information about supported features. After authorization, it can request Play, Pause, Stop, Previous, Next, Repeat Off/All/One, a normalized LiveSetPro Windows volume level and Karaoke Follow on/off. The volume value represents the app's own volume range, not Android volume or Windows system master volume. Technical session, request and song/target identifiers match requests to the correct connection and playback context and help reject outdated requests; they are not advertising or persistent device identifiers.

Remote data is exchanged directly between your paired devices and is not sent through a LiveSetPro developer cloud service. The developer does not receive, terminate or decrypt this Remote traffic through a LiveSetPro backend. The Wi-Fi name and password from a Direct QR are passed to Android's Wi-Fi connection API, not to a developer server.

Security and authorization

Remote uses an authenticated, encrypted local TLS/WebSocket (WSS) connection. The session QR or complete secure pairing code verifies the exact PC session certificate before Android sends its authentication token. There is no LiveSetPro cloud relay. Protect the pairing QR/code: someone who obtains the current code can connect to that session.

Every new Remote session starts Read-only. Android playback and Karaoke control requests remain disabled until the Windows user explicitly selects Allow control. Windows can revoke control at any time. Starting a new Remote session resets authorization to Read-only; pairing does not grant permanent control. Local Android font and alignment settings do not require playback-control permission.

Stopping Remote on the PC invalidates the session, and a new start creates fresh credentials and a new certificate. Direct works without Internet access; there is no cloud or public certificate-service dependency. Pairing uses numeric local IP addresses, without hostname lookup. Android, Google Play and your network provider operate independently under their own policies.

Android preferences and session retention

Android stores your lyric font-size and alignment preferences locally and your app-language preference through Android's locale mechanism. These presentation settings remain on Android, are separate from Windows presentation settings, and do not contain lyrics or pairing credentials. Clearing the app's local data removes its stored preferences.

Pairing credentials and received song/lyric state are held in app memory, not written as a permanent Android library, database, preference or saved-state file. Temporary session and command-correlation state may remain in memory while Remote is active. The app disables its data backup and device-transfer data extraction. Going to the background closes the active connection and releases any Direct network request. Direct pairing may remain in memory so the app can reconnect when you return. Disconnect, ending the app process, or clearing its ViewModel releases the session credentials. Re-pair after process termination.

Windows storage

Your Windows song library remains under your control. Repeat, LiveSetPro volume and Karaoke Follow choices can be saved locally by Windows, including when changed through authorized Android requests. Windows also keeps bounded temporary command/result state to avoid applying duplicate requests. Disconnecting Android does not delete your Windows library or reset these Windows preferences. No developer-held Remote account or cloud data exists to delete.

Local diagnostics

Connection/control status, counters and diagnostic codes may exist locally in app memory or device logs. There is no automatic LiveSetPro diagnostic-upload service. Pairing tokens, QR contents and lyrics are not intentionally uploaded to developer servers.

Your choices

You can leave Remote Read-only, revoke control on Windows, cancel scanning, disconnect at any time, and revoke Camera, Nearby devices or Location permission in Android Settings. Without Camera you can paste the complete secure pairing code. Copying this code on Windows places credentials on the system clipboard at your request; protect it as you would the QR. Without the permission required for Direct, you can use a shared local network.

Support and privacy requests

For help or privacy questions, contact livesetxpro@gmail.com. Include your Android version, app version and a description of the issue. Do not send pairing QR codes, Remote tokens, Wi-Fi passwords or private song content. If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so we can respond. This correspondence is separate from the app's local Remote session and remains in the email system used to handle your request. You can use the same address to request deletion of support correspondence; any applicable retention obligations will be explained in response.

Public website

The privacy/reviewer website has no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, forms or cookies added by us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes website requests and logs visitors' IP addresses for security, including when visitors are not signed in. See https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Website hosting is separate from the app's local phone-to-PC connection.

Changes

This policy describes the Android app and its Remote connection; your Windows music library stays under your control. Future changes to the app's data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date.
